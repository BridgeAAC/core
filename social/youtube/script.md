# BridgeAAC YouTube video: narration script

Length: 4:55. Read each line when the timecode comes up. Lines marked APP are spoken by BridgeAAC in the video, so stay quiet for those.

## 0:00 Opening

- `0:00` Everyone has something to say.
- `0:05` But for a lot of people, saying it out loud isn't easy. Or isn't possible at all.

## 0:13 Meet BridgeAAC

- `0:13` This is BridgeAAC. A free communication app that speaks for you, one picture at a time.

## 0:23 Why I built it

- `0:23` I built BridgeAAC for my sister. She uses an iPad to talk.
- `0:29` Apps like this can cost hundreds of dollars, and dedicated speech devices can cost thousands.
- `0:36` No family should have to pay for a voice. So BridgeAAC is free, for everyone.

## 0:45 Talking

- `0:45` Here's how it works. Every button is a word.
- `0:49` Tap the words you want, and they line up in the message bar. Then tap Speak.
- `0:57` *APP: "I want to play."*
- `0:59` The buttons are color coded, so people, actions and describing words are quick to find.

## 1:07 Folders

- `1:07` More specific words live in folders, like food, people and places.
- `1:16` *APP: "I want pizza."*
- `1:18` Open a folder, pick a word, and BridgeAAC takes you right back home.

## 1:25 Keyboard

- `1:25` If a word isn't on the board, open the keyboard and spell it.
- `1:31` Word prediction suggests whole words as you type, so it's faster.
- `1:38` *APP: "I want to go swimming."*

## 1:43 Symbols

- `1:43` Every button has a clear, friendly picture.
- `1:47` BridgeAAC comes with hundreds of its own symbols, drawn just for this app, and they all work without the internet.

## 1:59 Offline voices

- `1:59` You can choose the voice that fits.
- `2:03` Natural voices download once, then run right on the device. So BridgeAAC keeps talking on a plane, at school, or anywhere without Wi-Fi.

## 2:16 Make it yours

- `2:16` Every board can be made your own.
- `2:20` Tap Edit, pick any button, and change its word, its picture, or its color.
- `2:27` You can even add your own photos, like mom, a favorite toy, or the family dog.

## 2:37 Import and export

- `2:37` Already have boards from another AAC app?
- `2:41` BridgeAAC imports dot-C-E and Open Board files, and it can export them too. So you're never locked in.

## 2:52 Planner, moods and games

- `2:52` BridgeAAC helps with the rest of the day, too.
- `2:57` A visual planner shows the day step by step, with a star for every task that's done.
- `3:05` *APP: "Nice work! Wake Up and Stretch is done. You earned a star."*

## 3:12 Moods

- `3:12` Mood check-ins help people share how they feel, and get ideas for what might help.
- `3:20` *APP: "I feel happy!"*

## 3:25 Games

- `3:25` And simple games make practicing words and sentences fun.
- `3:35` *APP: "Awesome! I want water please."*

## 3:40 Accessibility

- `3:40` BridgeAAC adapts to the person using it.
- `3:43` There's a calm mode with soft colors, plus dark and high contrast themes.
- `3:50` Touch settings help with shaky hands, and switch scanning lets people speak even if they can't tap at all.

## 4:01 Languages and privacy

- `4:01` It comes in ten languages, including Spanish, French, Arabic, Chinese and Hindi.

## 4:11 Privacy

- `4:11` And it's private. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.
- `4:17` Your boards and messages stay on your device.

## 4:24 Every device

- `4:24` BridgeAAC runs on the devices families already have. iPad, iPhone, Android, Chromebook, and Windows, Mac and Linux computers.
- `4:34` On an iPad, just open bridgeaac.org in Safari and add it to your home screen.

## 4:42 Try it

- `4:42` BridgeAAC is free and open source.
- `4:46` Try it today at bridgeaac.org. Thanks for watching.
