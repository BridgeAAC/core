BridgeAAC is a free, open-source AAC app. Tap pictures to build sentences and hear them spoken. It works offline on iPad, iPhone, Android, Chromebook, Windows, Mac and Linux.

Try it: https://bridgeaac.org
Source code: https://github.com/BridgeAAC/core
