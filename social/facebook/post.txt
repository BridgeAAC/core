Important for iPad and iPhone families: how to get BridgeAAC today

BridgeAAC isn't in the App Store yet. Apple's review process takes a long time, and families shouldn't have to wait for it. You don't have to. You can install BridgeAAC on any iPad or iPhone right now, for free, in about 30 seconds:

1. Open Safari and go to bridgeaac.org
2. Tap the Share button (top right on iPad, bottom of the screen on iPhone)
3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
4. Tap Add

BridgeAAC now sits on your home screen like any other app. It opens full screen and works offline once it's installed. No account, no ads, nothing to pay.

Tip: back up your boards from time to time (Vocabularies, then Export) so you can move them to a new device whenever you like.

Questions or stuck on a step? Message us here or email support@bridgeaac.org. Please share this with anyone who has been waiting for the iOS app.
