BridgeAAC is now in testing on Android. Free, works offline, and built for people who use AAC and the families who support them.

Want to try it? It takes about 2 minutes:

1. Join the Google Group: [GROUP LINK]
   Use the Gmail account you use on your Android phone.
2. On that same phone, open this link while signed in with that account: [OPT-IN LINK]
3. Tap "Become a tester."
4. Tap "Download it on Google Play" and install.

Android phones and tablets only. iPad and iPhone users can install it from Safari instead: bridgeaac.org

Questions or something not working? Comment here or email support@bridgeaac.org. Feedback is very welcome while we test.
