BridgeAAC got a big update.

It's a free communication app for people who don't speak, or don't always speak, with boards you can customize and a voice that works without Wi-Fi.

What's new:
- Open boards straight from the Files app on iPad and iPhone
- .ce files open more reliably, with clearer messages when something's wrong
- Caregiver notes are encrypted with a passcode
- Online features are off until you turn them on
- A cleaner look, in light, dark, calm and high-contrast

No accounts. No ads. No tracking. The code is open, link in bio.
