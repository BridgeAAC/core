## 5. User & Caregiver Responsibility

- **Supervision & Daily Care**: Use of the Software by children, individuals with cognitive or developmental disabilities, and vulnerable persons must be supervised by a parent, legal guardian, licensed educator, or certified caregiver.
- **Independent Communication Backups**: Technology can fail, batteries deplete, screens crack, and devices get wet. Caregivers are strongly advised to maintain laminated paper communication backups and regular digital backups (via `.bridge` or `.ce` export).
- **Security & Device Passcodes**: The user and caregiver are solely responsible for setting strong device passcodes, Apple Guided Access, Android App Pinning, and BridgeAAC PIN locks to restrict unauthorized modifications.
