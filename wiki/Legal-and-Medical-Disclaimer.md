## 1. Assistive Technology Classification

BridgeAAC is independent software provided to facilitate augmentative communication, language development, and personal expression:
- **No Clinical Diagnosis**: The software, prebuilt starter vocabularies, mood tracking routines, and guides are for educational and communicative purposes only. They do not constitute clinical or medical advice.
- **Consult Licensed Clinicians**: Always consult certified Speech-Language Pathologists (SLPs), physicians, and occupational therapists for individualized communication, feeding, sensory, or behavioral intervention plans.
