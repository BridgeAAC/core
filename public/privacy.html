Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

BridgeAAC is a free communication app. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't collect, sell or share personal information.

What stays on your device

Boards, button labels, photos, recorded sounds, messages, phrase history, planner tasks, mood logs, the About Me page, caregiver notes and settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them. They leave your device only if you export a file yourself.

Caregiver notes are encrypted on the device when you set a notes passcode. The settings PIN is stored as a one-way hash.

Like any website or download, these services can see your IP address when they're contacted. They have their own privacy policies.

Children

BridgeAAC is used by children and adults. Because it doesn't collect personal information, it doesn't collect any from children. The two features that send text are off until a parent, caregiver or teacher turns them on.

Permissions

On iPhone and iPad the app may ask to use the camera or photo library, only when you add a picture to a button. It doesn't use the microphone, location or contacts.

Deleting your data

Delete the app, or use Settings → Privacy → Erase All Local Data, to remove everything. Because nothing is stored on our side, there is nothing for us to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
