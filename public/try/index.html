Try BridgeAAC

Free, offline AAC communication app. Choose your device below.

iPad & iPhone

Join the Apple TestFlight beta while the App Store version is in review.

  1. 1

    Install TestFlight

    Download Apple's free TestFlight app on your iPad or iPhone.

    Get TestFlight
  2. 2

    Join the BridgeAAC beta

    Open the public TestFlight link on your device to install automatically.

    Join TestFlight

Or skip TestFlight: open bridgeaac.org in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android

Join the Google Play closed beta.

We need 12 Android testers before Google lets us publish. Join the group and stay opted in for 14 days so BridgeAAC can go live on the Play Store for everyone.
  1. 1

    Enter your Google Play email

    Use the Gmail address signed into Google Play on your Android phone or tablet. You'll be added automatically — no emails, no group page.

  2. 2

    Opt in on Google Play

    Once added above, open the link below, tap Become a tester, and install BridgeAAC from Google Play.

    Opt in on Google Play

Need a direct file instead? Download the APK or open it in Chrome.
