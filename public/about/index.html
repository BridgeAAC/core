BridgeAAC: a free AAC app that works offline

BridgeAAC helps people who can't rely on speech say what they want to say. Tap pictures to build a sentence and BridgeAAC speaks it out loud. It's free, it runs on the devices families already have, and it keeps working without internet.

Start using BridgeAAC

What BridgeAAC does

Speaks what you tap

Build sentences from picture buttons and hear them spoken right away, with word prediction and a message bar.

Natural voices offline

Neural voices run on the device, so speech still works on a plane, at school or with no signal.

600+ original symbols

Clear pictures for core words, people, food, feelings, places, actions and more, made for BridgeAAC.

Ready-made boards

Start with a 42, 60, 84 or 108 button core vocabulary, then edit any button, page or color.

Bring your boards

Import .ce and Open Board (.obf, .obz) files, and export them again to share with your team.

Planner, moods and calm mode

Visual schedules with rewards, mood check-ins and a calm sensory mode for hard moments.

Works on the devices you already have

BridgeAAC runs on iPad, iPhone, Android phones and tablets, Chromebooks, and Windows, Mac and Linux computers. Install it once and it opens full screen like any other app.

How to install BridgeAAC

iPad and iPhone

  1. Open Safari and go to bridgeaac.org.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

BridgeAAC isn't in the App Store yet. Installing from Safari gives you the same app today.

Android and Chromebook

  1. Open bridgeaac.org in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install in the app, or choose Install app from the Chrome menu.

Windows, Mac and Linux

Open bridgeaac.org in Chrome or Edge and choose Install, or download the desktop app from GitHub releases.

Private by design

There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers. Boards, messages, planner tasks and caregiver notes stay on the device. Online speech and online symbol search are off unless you turn them on. Read the full privacy policy.

Free and open source

BridgeAAC is free for everyone and the code is open source under the MIT license at github.com/BridgeAAC/core. Donations help, but they're never required.

Questions

Is BridgeAAC free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions and no in-app purchases. Donations are welcome but never required.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Once it's installed, BridgeAAC works offline, including its natural voices.

How do I install it on an iPad or iPhone?

Open bridgeaac.org in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen and Add.

Can I bring boards from another AAC app?

BridgeAAC imports .ce files and Open Board (.obf and .obz) files, and it can export to them too.

Does BridgeAAC collect personal data?

No. There are no accounts, analytics or trackers, and your boards stay on your device.

Who is BridgeAAC for?

Children and adults who use AAC, including autistic people and people with apraxia, cerebral palsy, ALS, aphasia or other speech differences, and the families, teachers and speech therapists who support them.
